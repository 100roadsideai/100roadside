Dead battery: jump start or replacement?

How Treasure Coast drivers can tell when a jump start may be enough and when a battery replacement may be the better next step.

Call 100 RoadsideServices

A jump start may be enough

If lights or accessories were left on and the battery is otherwise healthy, a jump may get the vehicle running again.

Replacement may be more likely

Repeated no-starts, a battery that will not hold charge, visible damage, or an older battery can point toward replacement. Charging-system problems can also mimic a bad battery.

Before calling

Have the vehicle year, make, model, engine if known, and exact location ready. For mobile battery replacement, correct battery fitment matters.
