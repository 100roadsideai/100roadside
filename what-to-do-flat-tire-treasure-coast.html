What to do when you get a flat tire on the Treasure Coast

A practical local guide for drivers with a flat tire in Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, Stuart or nearby Treasure Coast roads.

Call 100 RoadsideServices

1. Get out of moving traffic if the vehicle can move safely

Use hazard lights and choose a stable location away from traffic. Avoid changing a tire in an unsafe travel lane or on soft, uneven ground.

2. Know whether you have a usable spare

Many newer vehicles do not carry a full-size spare. Check for a spare, inflator kit or wheel-lock key before roadside help arrives.

3. Send the useful details

Your exact location, vehicle year/make/model, tire position, whether the tire is shredded or simply losing air, and whether you have a spare help determine the right equipment.

When a plug may not be appropriate

Sidewall damage, a large puncture, exposed cords or severe tire damage generally require replacement rather than a simple tread repair.
