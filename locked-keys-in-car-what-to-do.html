Practical steps for a vehicle lockout in Port St. Lucie and the Treasure Coast, including when emergency services should come first.

Check for immediate danger first

If a child, vulnerable person or pet is in immediate danger inside a locked vehicle, contact emergency services rather than waiting for routine roadside service.

For a normal vehicle lockout

Confirm all doors and the trunk are actually locked, locate any spare-key option, and avoid improvised tools that can damage weather seals, trim or electronics.

What to send a roadside provider

Your exact location plus vehicle year, make and model help the technician prepare for the lockout.
